Welcome!

I am an early-career researcher who uses social theory and mixed methods to understand the structural determinants of homelessness, the governance of homelessness by local service providers, and the impact of policy interventions on homelessness. My award-winning research has been published in peer-reviewed journals like European Journal on Homelessness; Housing Studies; Housing, Theory and Society; International Journal on Homelessness; Journal of Social Distress and Homelessness; Housing and Society; and Urban Geography.

I earned my PhD in sociology from the University of Wisconsin-Madison in 2021, where my research was supported by the Institute for Research on Poverty, CROWE Scholarship, Mellon-Wisconsin Fellowship, and Advanced Opportunity Fellowship. I then earned an MS in Spatial Planning and Real Estate Development from Heriot-Watt University in 2023, which was financed in part by the Saltire Scholarship from Scottish Government. Since 2021, I have held postdoc research positions at the University of Glasgow, Manchester Metropolitan University, and Wrexham University.

You can contact me at garrett.grainger@wrexham.ac.uk or g.grainger@mmu.ac.uk.
